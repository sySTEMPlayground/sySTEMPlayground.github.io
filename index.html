sySTEM Playground

Free enrichment program whose novel and relevant courses empower students to pioneer in STEM.

What makes us different

Applicability

Our course material has real-world applications that are hot topics among technology today. Instead of telling our students what we teach them is important, we show them.

Unique Courses

Each subject we teach is not typically taught to our target age group. But we think these topics are introduced too late to have a meaningful impact for students, so we introduce them now.

Challenging content

We teach students to use STEM as a playground: you first climb the ladder before you go down the slide: learning is fun because of the work you put into it, not despite it.

Q&A

How long does a class run?

All classes run for around 1 hour per session.

How often do classes happen?

Depending on the class topic, classes can meet once to twice per week. Math classes run twice a week, whereas Computer Science and Natural Science classes tend to meet once a week.

How old are sySTEM Playground's students?

Our teaching range is from 4th to 8th grade, but the recommended age for classes is under the course description.

Is sySTEM Playground free?

Yes! Classes are free of charge, meaning families can enjoy as much STEM as they want and not need to feel worried about the cost. We are bringing novelty and relevance to STEM education without the price tag.
